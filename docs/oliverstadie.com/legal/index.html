Hosting and technical provision of the website

This website is provided via GitHub Pages. When you access the website, technically necessary connection and usage data is processed, in particular your IP address, date and time of access, requested content, browser and device information, and additional technical log data.

The processing takes place for the secure and stable provision of the website and for the prevention of misuse and security incidents on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the secure operation and technical delivery of this website.

For this technical provision we use GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub B.V. / GitHub, Inc. GitHub processes certain technical data under its own data protection responsibility. Processing may take place in the EU, the United States, and other countries in which GitHub, affiliated companies, or subprocessors operate. According to GitHub’s current information, relevant third-country transfers are based in particular on Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Technical log data is retained by GitHub for a short period for security and operational purposes and is then deleted in accordance with GitHub’s internal retention rules or retained only to the extent necessary for legal or operational reasons.

Further information is available in GitHub’s privacy information and GitHub Pages data-collection notice: GitHub Privacy Statement, GitHub Pages – Data Collection.

Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the data you provide to us, in particular your email address, other contact data, the content of your message, and related communication metadata.

Where your inquiry is aimed at entering into or performing a contract, the processing is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. In all other cases, we process your data on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the efficient handling of inquiries, business communication, and documentation for the assertion, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

We use Gmail from Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, to operate our email mailbox. According to Google’s current information, data may be processed via global infrastructure, including in the United States. For relevant third-country transfers, Google refers in particular to Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

For sending emails via our own domain, we additionally use SMTP2GO as a technical email delivery service provider. According to SMTP2GO’s current information, the account used here is hosted in the EU. SMTP2GO is a service of Sand Dune Mail Ltd., 96-106 Manchester Street, Christchurch 8011, New Zealand. To the extent personal data is processed outside the EU/EEA, we base such transfers on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for New Zealand.

Spam, misdirected, and clearly irrelevant messages are deleted promptly. Other communication is generally stored until the relevant matter has been concluded. Ordinary business correspondence without statutory retention duties is then generally kept for three years from the end of the calendar year of the last relevant contact for documentation and legal-claims purposes. Messages relevant under commercial, tax, or accounting law are stored for the applicable statutory retention periods, usually six or eight years from the end of the relevant calendar year. Deleted Gmail messages typically remain in Trash for up to 30 days. According to SMTP2GO’s current documentation, activity data on the free plan remains visible for five days, and activity or header data is deleted after 35 days by default. We do not use paid archiving features for longer-term storage of full message bodies and attachments.

Further information is available at: Google Privacy Policy, Google Data Transfer Frameworks, SMTP2GO Privacy Policy.
